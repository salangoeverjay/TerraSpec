# Panabo City Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) C.Y. 2020-2029, Volume 1

Source document: CLUP Panabo City (1).pdf (City Government of Panabo, adopted with the Zoning Ordinance). Page numbers below are the printed page numbers ("Page X of 214").

## City profile
- Total land area: 25,123 hectares. [CLUP p.144]
- Population: 209,230 (2020 PSA); projected 265,924 by 2029. [CLUP p.143]
- Population density: 833 persons per km² city-wide; 4,156 per km² in urban barangays; 377 per km² in rural barangays. [CLUP p.143]
- 40 barangays: 11 urban and 29 rural. Urban barangays have 126,003 people, 60% of the city population (2020 PSA). [CLUP p.15]
- The CLUP maps were prepared in QGIS following HLURB standard guidelines. [CLUP p.142]

## Settlement pattern (proposed)
- Major growth center: expanded from five to seven urban barangays. The existing central business district (CBD) covers Sto. Niño, San Francisco, New Pandan and Gredu; J.P. Laurel, Cagangohan and San Pedro are added to the CBD. [CLUP p.141]
- The city plans to convert the public market in New Pandan into a modern commercial complex; development spreads along the national highway starting at J.P. Laurel. [CLUP p.141]
- Minor growth centers (six): New Visayas, Datu Abdul Dadia, Salvacion, San Vicente, Quezon and A.O. Floirendo. [CLUP p.141]
- Emerging barangays: Tagpore, Malativas, Kauswagan, Mabunao and Southern Davao; they support the satellite barangays through services. [CLUP p.141]
- Satellite barangays: the remaining barangays within the perimeter of the emerging barangays; their role is to provide agricultural crops and other services. [CLUP p.141]

## Development constraints (Table LW-1)
The CLUP treats these natural and man-made constraints as areas where the City Government cannot introduce projects or improvements. [CLUP p.143]
- Irrigated and irrigable lands: 530.29 ha (2.24%).
- CARP-covered areas: 6,272.93 ha (26.52%).
- Flood-prone areas (very high susceptibility, near Lasang River): 536.24 ha in the text, 512.22 ha in Table LW-1 (2.17%).
- Erosion potential areas: 2,340.93 ha (9.90%).
- SAFDZ: 13,998.66 ha (59.18%), tabulated as 12,101.44 ha when overlaid with irrigated and irrigable lands.
- Total constrained: 23,655.03 ha. Source of the table: Department of Agrarian Reform, Panabo, Davao. [CLUP p.143]

## Urbanizable land
- Urbanizable land is land suitable for urban expansion based on soil suitability studies, slopes, soil type and erosion potential, development trends and constraints. [CLUP p.144]
- Urbanizable land = total land area − constrained areas = 25,123 − 23,655.03 = 1,467.97 ha. [CLUP p.144]
