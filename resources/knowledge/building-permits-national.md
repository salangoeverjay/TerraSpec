# Building in the Philippines: the general national process

Source: national laws that apply in every Philippine city, not a Panabo City ordinance. Cite the law named in each line, e.g. [PD 1096] or [PD 1067]. Panabo City's own requirements, fees and forms are set by the city: always tell the user to confirm with the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO) and the Office of the Building Official.

## Permits for a building
- Locational Clearance (also called zoning clearance): confirms that the planned use is allowed in the zone under the city's zoning ordinance. It is issued by the city's zoning administrator, in Panabo City the CPDO, and is needed before a building permit. [Zoning ordinance practice under HLURB/DHSUD guidelines]
- Building Permit: required before building, altering or repairing a building. It is issued by the Office of the Building Official under the National Building Code. [PD 1096]
- Certificate of Occupancy: required before a new building may be used; it is issued by the Building Official after construction is finished. [PD 1096]
- A barangay clearance from the barangay where the building will stand is commonly required by the city as part of the application. [City practice; confirm with the CPDO]

## Building near rivers, streams and the sea
- Land along the banks of rivers and streams and the shores of seas and lakes is subject to a legal easement for public use: 3 m in urban areas, 20 m in agricultural areas and 40 m in forest areas. No building may be put up inside the easement. [PD 1067, Water Code of the Philippines, Art. 51]

## What TerraSpec can and cannot tell
- TerraSpec shows the zoning class, hazards and suitability of a barangay; it does not issue clearances or permits and does not know parcel boundaries, setbacks, building heights or fees. Those come from the official Zoning Ordinance and the National Building Code through the CPDO and the Office of the Building Official.
