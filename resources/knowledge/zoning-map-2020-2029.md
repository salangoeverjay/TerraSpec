# Panabo City Integrated Zoning Map C.Y. 2020-2029

Source: Panabo City Integrated Zoning Map C.Y. 2020-2029, prepared by the City Planning and Development Office with the technical assistance of DHSUD XI; coordinate system WGS84 / UTM Zone 51N. Base source: Barangay Development Plan. The map notes that its boundaries are not authoritative and are for mapping purposes only.

## How TerraSpec uses the map
- TerraSpec digitized the map image into polygons. C-2 and C-3, I-1 to I-3, and Parks and Cemetery are merged because the image distinguishes them only by hatching. Areas are approximate (about 10-20 m positional accuracy). [TerraSpec data]
- Zoning class hectares per barangay are TerraSpec data, computed from the digitized map within PSA 2023 barangay boundaries. [TerraSpec data]
- The allowed uses per zoning class used in TerraSpec's "zoning compliance" score are provisional values based on the HLURB model zoning ordinance, not the Panabo Zoning Ordinance text. They must be confirmed against the official ordinance. [TerraSpec data]
